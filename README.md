BrewBean Coffee Shop Sales Analytics
Project README
1. Project Overview
BrewBean Coffee Shop Sales Analytics is a Business Analytics project developed to analyze coffee shop sales, outlet performance, customer purchasing behaviour, product performance, and profitability.

The project uses cleaned business data to identify sales trends, top-performing products and outlets, busy hours, customer behaviour, and payment preferences.
2. Objectives
• Analyze total revenue and sales performance
• Identify top-performing outlets
• Identify top-selling beverages and food items
• Analyze busiest hours and peak sales periods
• Understand customer purchasing behaviour
• Analyze promotion and payment-mode performance
• Identify profitable products and business areas
• Provide business recommendations
3. Dataset
The project contains the following datasets:

• Sales / Transaction Data
• Customer Data
• Product Data
• Outlet Data
• Promotion Data

The datasets were cleaned before analysis by checking missing values, duplicate records, data types, inconsistent values, and formatting issues.
4. Data Model
The cleaned datasets were connected using relationships in the Excel Data Model to support integrated analysis.
5. Analysis & Dashboard
The Excel dashboard includes key performance indicators, PivotTables, charts, and slicers to analyze:

• Total Revenue
• Total Sales
• Total Orders
• Outlet Performance
• Top Products
• Busiest Time
• Customer Analysis
• Payment Mode
• Promotion Performance

8. Business Insights
The analysis helps management understand sales performance, customer behaviour, product demand, outlet performance, and operational patterns.
9. Recommendations
Based on the analysis, BrewBean can improve business performance by focusing on high-performing products and outlets, optimizing peak-hour operations, improving promotional campaigns, and using customer purchasing patterns for better decision-making.
